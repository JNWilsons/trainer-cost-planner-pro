# 📊 TrainerCompass – Dynamic Training Cost Intelligence Suite

Welcome to **TrainerCompass**, the evolution of spreadsheet-based training cost planning. While traditional templates like MCT-Kalkulationsvorlage provide a static snapshot of expenses, TrainerCompass transforms your cost estimation workflow into a **living, breathing decision-support system** designed for the modern freelance trainer who juggles multiple clients, currencies, and certification bodies.

Think of TrainerCompass not as a digital ledger, but as a **navigation system** for your training business—it doesn't just show you the road; it anticipates potholes, suggests alternate routes when a client changes scope, and ensures you never arrive at the end of a quarter with an unexpected budget deficit.

---

## 🧭 Why TrainerCompass Exists

The original MCT-Kalkulationsvorlage serves as an excellent starting point—a clean spreadsheet that captures line-item costs for Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) courses. However, the training industry has evolved beyond single-currency, single-format workshops. Modern freelance trainers deliver:

- Hybrid sessions (physical + virtual simultaneous)
- Multi-timezone cohorts requiring 24/7 coordination
- Custom courseware development tied to specific client LMS platforms
- Tiered pricing models (per participant vs. flat rate vs. revenue share)

TrainerCompass addresses these realities by wrapping the core cost estimation engine inside a **responsive, multilingual, API-ready** framework. It doesn't replace your existing spreadsheet; it elevates it to a strategic asset.

---

## 🚀 Core Capabilities That Redefine Training Budgets

### 1. **Scenario Weaving Engine** (Dynamic What-If Analysis)
Instead of static columns, TrainerCompass lets you **weave multiple scenarios** simultaneously. Adjust participant count, room rental duration, or trainer travel distance and watch the entire cost fabric re-weave in real-time. Each scenario retains its own context, allowing you to compare "Minimum Viable Delivery" against "Premium Concierge Experience" side-by-side.

### 2. **Cost Elasticity Dashboard**
Calculate not just total costs but **cost elasticity**—how sensitive your profit margin is to each variable. Discover that a 10% increase in catering impacts your margin twice as much as a 10% increase in venue rental. This insight transforms negotiations with venues and suppliers.

### 3. **Multi-Currency Hedging Module**
Trainers working across borders face FX volatility. TrainerCompass includes a **built-in currency stabilizer** that uses rolling 90-day average exchange rates, not just today's spot rate. This prevents unpleasant surprises when invoicing international clients.

### 4. **Regulatory Compliance Tracker**
With training regulations shifting across regions, the suite includes a compliance layer that flags potential VAT implications, certification body fee changes, and insurance requirements—ensuring your cost baseline includes **mandatory governance overheads**.

### 5. **Legacy Import Bridge**
Have a beloved MCT-Kalkulationsvorlage file with years of historical data? TrainerCompass includes a **structured import wizard** that maps your existing spreadsheet columns into the new data model, preserving historical comparability.

## 🔮 The Philosophical Shift: From Estimation to Prediction

Traditional cost calculation treats each training as an isolated event. TrainerCompass adopts a **continuous learning posture**. Every time you complete a training, the system subtly recalibrates its baseline assumptions based on your actual historical costs (if you opt into anonymized telemetry). After three trainings, the tool begins suggesting budget cushions tailored to your specific patterns—perhaps you consistently underestimate printing costs for hands-on labs, or you over-allocate for coffee breaks during afternoon cohorts.

This isn't magic; it's **accumulated contextual intelligence** applied to your unique training style.

## 🌍 Multilingual & International Readiness

The global training market expects tools that speak the local language of finance. TrainerCompass's interface supports:

- **English (UK/US variants)** for core financial terminology
- **German** with proper distinction between *Brutto/Netto* calculations
- **French** with Canadian and European VAT handling nuances
- **Spanish** with LatAm exchange rate conventions

The calculation engine always operates on a **canonical numerical core**; language only affects presentation layers. This ensures that a cost breakdown translated from German to English preserves exact decimal precision and rounding rules.

Always maintain a **safety margin** equal to at least 8-12% of your total estimated budget for unforeseen operational exigencies.

The software author(s) and contributors accept no liability for business decisions made solely on the basis of TrainerCompass outputs. Treat every calculated figure as a **starting point for professional judgment**, not as an immutable destiny.
